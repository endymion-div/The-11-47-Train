# 🚂 The 11:47 Train

> **“The train has 90 seconds to hide the truth.”**

**The 11:47 Train** is an original cinematic **2D/2.5D browser-based detective mystery game** set aboard a luxury overnight train during a violent storm.

At exactly **11:47 PM**, every light on the train goes out for 90 seconds.

When the lights return, a passenger is found dead inside a locked compartment.

The train never stopped.  
The windows were sealed.  
Nobody could have entered or escaped.

Yet everyone has an alibi.

And almost everyone is lying.

Your job is not simply to find **who** committed the crime — but to reconstruct **exactly what happened during those 90 seconds**.

# 📖 Story

The **Silver Meridian** is traveling through the countryside on a stormy night.

At 11:20 PM, private consulting detective **Elias Vane** receives an anonymous note:

> **“At 11:47 PM, someone on this train will die.”**

At first, Elias assumes it is merely a threat.

At exactly **11:47 PM**, however, the entire train loses power.

For **90 seconds**, the Silver Meridian disappears into darkness.

When the lights return, a passenger is discovered dead inside:

**Carriage 7 — Compartment 4.**

The compartment is locked.

The windows are sealed.

The train has never stopped.

Six people had access to the carriage.

Every suspect has an alibi.

But every alibi contains something that doesn't quite fit.

# ⏱️ The 90-Second Mystery

The central mystery revolves around the blackout.

```text
11:46:30
Train operating normally
        ↓
11:47:00
BLACKOUT BEGINS
        ↓
11:47:15
Unknown movement detected
        ↓
11:47:30
Sound heard from Carriage 7
        ↓
11:48:00
BLACKOUT ENDS
        ↓
11:48:03
Body discovered
```

The player must determine what happened during each interval.

The solution cannot simply be guessed.

It must be **reconstructed from evidence**.


# 🌑 The Blackout

The 90-second blackout is the centerpiece of the game.

When the blackout begins:

- Visual information is drastically reduced.
- The train becomes almost completely dark.
- Ambient audio changes.
- Player interactions become limited.
- Sounds become critical pieces of evidence.

The player may hear:

- Footsteps
- Breathing
- A distant metallic sound
- A door closing
- Something falling
- Train movement
- Someone calling out

Possible visual events can include:

- A passing silhouette
- A door opening
- An object falling
- Movement between compartments

The blackout does **not** directly reveal the murderer.

Its purpose is to create questions that the player must answer afterward.

# 🎯 Core Design Philosophy

**The 11:47 Train** is built around one principle:

> **The player should solve the mystery — not the game.**

The game should avoid simply telling players what happened.

Instead, players must:

1. Observe their surroundings.
2. Collect evidence.
3. Question suspects.
4. Detect contradictions.
5. Compare alibis.
6. Reconstruct the blackout.
7. Build theories.
8. Test those theories.
9. Connect seemingly unrelated clues.
10. Make the final accusation.

# 🧠 What Makes The 11:47 Train Different?

Unlike a conventional hidden-object mystery, the game focuses on **logical reconstruction**.

The important question is not simply:

> “Who looks suspicious?”

It is:

> **“Can you prove what happened during 90 seconds when nobody could see?”**

A footprint, a sound, a missing ticket, a false statement and the movement of the train may appear unrelated.

Until they aren't.
